Retail Sales Performance Analysis (2024)

I wanted to find out where this business makes its profit and where it quietly loses it.

SQLAdvanced ExcelPower BIData CleaningKPI Analysis
About the data: This is a practice project. The dataset is simulated (1,500 orders from 2024) and was prepared for learning, so it does not belong to any real company.

1. Business problem

Sales in this retail business looked healthy, but the overall profit margin was only 14.5%. I wanted to understand what was pulling the margin down and what the business could do about it.

3. Approach

  • Cleaning in Excel: I checked for duplicates and blanks, fixed the date format, and confirmed that sales = price × quantity × (1 − discount) for every row.
  • Analysis in SQL: I grouped sales, profit and margin by month, region, category, segment and discount level.
  • Reporting in Power BI: I built KPI cards, a monthly trend and breakdown charts, with slicers for region and category.

5. Sample SQL

-- Margin by discount band
SELECT discount,
       ROUND(SUM(sales), 0)                      AS total_sales,
       ROUND(SUM(profit), 0)                     AS total_profit,
       ROUND(SUM(profit) * 100.0 / SUM(sales), 1) AS margin_pct,
       COUNT(*)                                  AS orders
FROM   retail_sales
GROUP  BY discount
ORDER  BY discount;

-- Monthly sales and running total
SELECT month, monthly_sales,
       SUM(monthly_sales) OVER (ORDER BY month) AS running_sales
FROM (
  SELECT EXTRACT(MONTH FROM order_date) AS month,
         SUM(sales) AS monthly_sales
  FROM   retail_sales
  GROUP  BY 1
) m;

6. What I would suggest

  • Limit discounts to 10% unless a manager approves more, since 20% and above wipe out the margin.
  • Look closely at Furniture pricing and costs. It sells a lot but earns very little.
  • Plan Q4 early so the busy season is not driven by heavy discounts.
  • Push Technology and Office Supplies, which have the best margins.
  • Check the East region for pricing or cost problems.
